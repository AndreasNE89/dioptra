Dioptra — Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 July 2026

Dioptra does not collect any data. It has no analytics, no accounts, and no telemetry. It also has no network access: the app is sandboxed by macOS and does not request the network entitlement, so it is not permitted to make an outbound connection even in principle. Nothing you do in the app leaves your Mac.

What Dioptra does with your files

Dioptra is a file manager, so it reads the folders you point it at in order to show you what is inside them. That reading happens entirely on your Mac, and the results are only ever drawn on your screen.

Dioptra is sandboxed by macOS and starts with access to nothing. It can only read a folder after you have chosen that folder yourself in the standard macOS folder picker. macOS records your choice as a security-scoped bookmark, stored in Dioptra's own private container on your Mac, so the app can still open that folder the next time you launch it. You can revoke any of these grants from within the app.

Dioptra never copies, uploads, transmits, or sends the contents of your files anywhere. It has no server to send them to.

Features that analyse your files

Two features look at your files more closely than simply listing them:

  • Reclaim Space finds large, old, and duplicate files so you can decide what to delete.
  • Organize suggests folders to group files into, based on their names, kinds, and dates.

Both run entirely on your Mac, using Apple's on-device frameworks. Neither sends file names, file contents, or anything else off your device.

Data collected

None.

Dioptra collects no personal data, no usage data, no diagnostics, and no identifiers. This is also what is declared on Dioptra's App Store product page under “App Privacy”.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in macOS System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports. That sharing is controlled entirely by Apple and by your own system setting; Dioptra does not gather or transmit anything itself. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings.

Children

Dioptra is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.
